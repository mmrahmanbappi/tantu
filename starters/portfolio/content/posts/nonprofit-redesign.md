## Starting point

The old site had small grey text and a menu with twenty links.

## Changes

- Cut the menu to five clear sections
- Raised text size and contrast to meet accessibility guidelines
- Put the volunteer sign up on the home page

## Why it matters

Many of the people who visit this site read on small phones. Every change made the site easier for them.
