## The brief

The club posted events in a group chat, and people missed them. They wanted one place to see what is coming up.

## Process

I interviewed eight members, sketched three ideas on paper, and tested the best one with a clickable prototype.

## Outcome

Sign ups for the first month doubled compared to the term before.
