## The problem

The bakery only had a social media page. Customers kept calling to ask about opening hours and prices.

## What I did

I designed a one page site with the menu, hours and a map link. It loads in under a second on a slow connection.

## Result

Phone calls about hours dropped, and the owner updates the menu herself by editing one text file.
