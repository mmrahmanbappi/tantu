Membership is free for all students. You do not need your own camera.

## How to join

1. Come to any Wednesday meeting
2. Fill in the short sign up form with a committee member
3. Join our group chat to hear about photo walks

Questions? Email [photoclub@example.edu](mailto:photoclub@example.edu).
