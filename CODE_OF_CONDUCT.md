# Code of conduct

tantu is built by students, teachers and volunteers from many countries. We want everyone to feel welcome, whatever their experience, background or English level.

## We expect

- Kindness and patience, especially with people who are new to programming or open source
- Feedback about the work, not the person
- Clear, simple language, so people who read English as a second language can follow
- Respect for people's time and privacy

## We do not accept

- Insults, harassment or personal attacks
- Sexual, violent or discriminatory language or images
- Sharing someone's private information without permission
- Repeatedly pushing a discussion after being asked to stop

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break these rules. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through GitHub.
